Privacy

How we handle your family's information

隐私说明

You're trusting us with details about someone you love. Here is what we collect, why, and what we do with it, in plain language.

What we collect

On the waitlist form: your name and contact details, your parent's first name, the first part of their postal code, their language, their diet needs, and anything you choose to tell us.

When you sign up for a plan: your parent's address and phone number, emergency contacts, building entry details, allergies and diet needs, and food preferences. We collect these by phone, not on the website.

When drivers or restaurants apply: the contact details and answers in their form.

Why we collect it

  • To contact you about the pilot and your plan
  • To plan routes and choose partner restaurants
  • To prepare meals safely, including allergies and diet needs
  • To follow our safety protocol if your parent doesn't answer

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who sees it

  • Your parent's driver sees their name, address, phone, entry details and emergency contacts, so they can deliver and follow the protocol.
  • Partner restaurants see your parent's name or initials, their order, and their allergies and diet needs.
  • Our service providers store data for us, such as our form, payment and email tools. They may store data outside Canada.
  • Emergency services, if a driver calls 911 under our protocol.

Website analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to count page visits and see how fast pages load. They don't use cookies, and they don't collect your name, email or anything you type into a form.

How long we keep it

Waitlist details are kept until the pilot is set up, or until you ask us to remove them. Customer details are kept while you use Warm Bowl and deleted within 12 months after you stop, unless we need to keep a record for tax or legal reasons.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. Email warmbowlmeals@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and tell current customers.
