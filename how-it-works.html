How it works

Set it up once. Then dinner just shows up.

一次设定，晚餐准时送到。

Families set preferences once. Your parent keeps the fun part, choosing what to eat, and nobody has to place an order every day.

When you sign up

We ask once, by message or a short form, and you can change anything later.

Food they like

Favourite dishes and restaurants, dislikes, and how spicy they like it.

Diet needs

Allergies, low salt, low sugar, low fat, soft texture, or smaller portions.

Who to call

You first, then a backup contact. If they live in a building, the entry code or concierge.

How to reach them

Their phone number, which language they prefer, and anything the driver should know.

At a glance

  • Set nights: Tuesday and Thursday
  • Dinner window: 5:00 to 6:15 pm
  • Choose by 8 pm the night before
  • Cancel free until 2 pm the same day
  • Pause with 24 hours' notice
  • Charged weekly, in advance, by card
Each week

Choosing dinner

We work with two or three restaurants in one plaza, one restaurant per night. Everyone on the route gets dinner on the same set evenings, Tuesday and Thursday to start. That keeps the route short and the food hot, while your parent still gets variety through the week.

1

See the day's menu

Each delivery day has a short list of dishes from that day's restaurant. We send it by text, WhatsApp or WeChat.

2

Pick by 8 pm

You or your parent choose the night before. If nobody picks, we bring one of their saved favourites.

3

Or tell the driver

No smartphone? Your parent can tell the driver at the door: "Congee on Friday, please." It gives them a say, and it's part of the check-in.

Delivery night

What happens at the door

The driver picks up the meals between 4:30 and 5:00 pm, checks the temperature, and packs them in insulated bags. Every stop is on one short route in your parent's neighbourhood.

At the door, the driver hands the meal to your parent in person, says hello, and notices how they seem. If nobody answers, they follow our no-answer protocol.

Afterwards

A short note to you

Warm BowlFri, 5:41 pm

Handed to your dad at 5:38. He said the wonton soup on Wednesday was too salty, so we'll flag that with the restaurant.

Daniel, his driver

A note tells you the meal was handed over and how your parent seemed. It can't prove they ate it. If you'd like to know something specific, tell us and we'll try to include it.

Changes

Cancelling, pausing and hospital stays

Life with an older parent changes quickly. The rules are simple so you don't have to think about them.

Skip one dinner

Text us, message us on WhatsApp or WeChat, or tell the driver. Free until 2 pm that day, when we send the order to the restaurant. After that, the meal is charged.

Pause for a while

Travel or family visiting? Pause with 24 hours' notice and restart whenever you like.

Hospital stay

Tell us and we pause straight away, no charge, until they're home. Then we'll check in about any new diet instructions.

Late or cold meal

If a meal arrives more than 30 minutes after the window, or isn't hot, we refund it. No forms.

Stop for good

Let us know any time. Nothing is charged after the current week.

Later on

More dinners, and lunches, when you're ready

We're starting with restaurant dinners a few times a week. As our routes fill, we'll add more partner restaurants and more nights, up to dinner every night, and then weekday lunches between 11:30 am and 12:45 pm. It stays real restaurant food, at the same $20 a meal. We'll never switch to a central kitchen.

With everyday plans, we'll plan the week from your parent's favourite dishes across our partner restaurants and send it to you on Sunday night. You only swap what you want to change. On a bad-appetite day, a light option like congee or soup can be swapped in until 9 am.
