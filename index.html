Why we started

“Did she eat today?”

If you're asking yourself that most evenings, you're not alone. Many of us order takeout for a parent on an app, pay around $30 a meal, and then wonder whether it arrived hot, whether they opened the door, and whether they liked it.

Warm Bowl is for families who already do this and want it to be simpler and more reliable. You set it up once. A familiar driver handles the rest, and you hear back every time.

01 / How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself

No daily ordering for you, and nothing new for your parent to learn.

1

Tell us about them

Favourite dishes, spice level, allergies, low salt or soft food, and the best way to reach them. You do this once.

2

They pick dinner

Each delivery day has a short menu from that day's restaurant. You or your parent choose by 8 pm the night before, by WhatsApp, WeChat or phone. Or they just tell the driver at the door.

3

The same driver brings it

Hot, on set evenings between 5:00 and 6:15 pm, handed to your parent in person. You get a short note after each visit.

The full details

02 / The driver

The person at the door matters as much as the food

We hire for patience and warmth, not speed. Your parent sees the same face each time, and that person knows what to do if something seems wrong.

Same driver, every visit

One driver per route, ideally someone who speaks Cantonese or Mandarin. Seniors get to know them, and they get to know your parent.

Checked and trained

Every driver passes a police vulnerable sector check before their first shift, and gets basic dementia-awareness training.

A plan if no one answers

A written step-by-step protocol: knock, call your parent, call you, call your backup, and call 911 if there are signs of distress. You see it before you sign up.

Clear limits

Drivers never go inside, never accept money or gifts, and never leave food outside unattended.

Meals on Wheels prices vary by agency and eligibility. If your parent qualifies and is happy with weekday lunches, it's a great option, and we're glad to point you to it.

Pay it forward

Every 20 meals sold, a senior in need eats free

Some seniors who would love a hot dinner can't afford one. So for every 20 meals families buy, we give a free meal to a senior who needs it, found through a local community agency or Meals on Wheels referrals.

Want to do more? Add Sponsor a meal to any week for $5, and it goes toward more free dinners.

A note from the founder

This started with my grandma

My dad orders dinner for my grandma a few times a week because she doesn't have a smartphone. It costs about $30 a meal, finding good food takes time, and he still worries about whether she ate.

I'm a student. My grandparents live in Richmond Hill and we visit often, so that's where I'm starting Warm Bowl: small and local, with a handful of founding families. For now an adult in my family drives, and I'm the one at the door. If that sounds like what your family needs, I'd love to hear from you.
