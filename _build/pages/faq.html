Is it right for us?

Why not just use Meals on Wheels?

If your parent qualifies and is happy with weekday lunches from a set menu, Meals on Wheels is a great, affordable choice, and you should use it. We're for what it usually doesn't cover: dinners, food from restaurants your parent already loves, the same driver every time, and families outside a program's service area. Some families use both.

Why not DoorDash or Uber Eats?

Apps are great for a one-off order. For a parent living alone, they mean ordering every time, a different courier each time, food left at the door, and no one checking in. With us, dinner arrives on a schedule, from the same driver, handed over in person, with a note to you afterwards. It also usually costs less than app prices plus fees and tips. If none of that matters to you, an app may suit you better.

Where do you deliver?

We're starting with one route in Bayview Hill, Richmond Hill, for seniors within about 2 to 3 km of our partner restaurants. A tight area is what keeps the food hot. If you're further away, please still join the waitlist. We choose our next neighbourhoods based on where families are waiting.

When do you start?

With a small test for 3 to 5 families in spring 2027, then an 8-week summer pilot for 10 to 15 seniors in July and August. We'll call everyone on the waitlist before then.

Which nights do you deliver?

Tuesday and Thursday evenings, between 5:00 and 6:15 pm, with Saturday added later for 3 Treats. Everyone on the route gets dinner on the same nights. That's how we keep the route short, the food hot and the price at $20.

Who's behind Warm Bowl?

A student whose grandparents live in Richmond Hill, with family helping on the first routes. We're open about being small and new. Read our story.

Safety and the driver

What happens if my parent doesn't answer the door?

The driver waits a few minutes, calls your parent, then calls you, then your backup contact or the concierge. If there are signs of distress, or nobody can be reached when your parent should be home, they call 911. The meal comes back with the driver, and you aren't charged. See the full protocol.

Who are the drivers?

People we know and trust, paid hourly. Every driver passes a police vulnerable sector check, has insurance that covers delivery, and is trained in our protocol and basic dementia awareness. Where we can, they speak Cantonese or Mandarin.

Will the driver come inside?

No. Drivers hand the meal over at the door and never go inside. They also never accept money, tips or gifts.

Does the note prove my parent ate?

No, and we won't claim it does. The note tells you the meal was handed over, when, and how your parent seemed. If there's something specific you'd like to know, tell us and we'll try to include it.

The food

Isn't this just takeout?

We pick home-style dishes like congee, steamed fish, soups and tofu rather than fried food, and start with a few dinners a week rather than every meal. Each partner restaurant offers lighter versions of a few dishes, with less salt and oil, softer textures and smaller portions. More about the food.

Can you handle allergies and special diets?

Yes for allergies, low salt, low sugar, low fat, soft texture and smaller portions. They're recorded once and printed on every label, and the restaurant confirms each order. For strict medical diets, please talk to us first and we'll be honest about whether we can meet it.

Who chooses the food, me or my parent?

Whoever you like. Many families let the parent choose, even when the family pays. Your parent can tell the driver at the door what they want next time, or you can choose by WhatsApp, WeChat or phone by 8 pm the night before.

Do you deliver lunch?

Not yet. We're starting with dinners. Once our routes are running, we plan to add weekday lunches between 11:30 am and 12:45 pm, at the same $20 a meal. Tick "weekday lunches" on the waitlist form if you'd want them.

What if they don't want to eat that night?

Cancel before 2 pm and it's free. On everyday plans later on, you'll also be able to switch to a light option like congee or soup until 9 am.

Payment and changes

How do I pay?

By card, weekly, in advance. Prices are before HST, so a $20 meal comes to $22.60. Your parent never handles money with the driver.

What if my parent goes into hospital?

Tell us and we pause straight away at no charge, until they're home.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes. Skip a meal free until 2 pm on the day. Pause for travel with 24 hours' notice. Stop any time, and nothing is charged after the current week.

What is the free meal for every 20 sold?

For every 20 meals families buy, we give a free hot dinner to a senior in need, found through a local community agency or Meals on Wheels referrals. It's built into every plan at no extra cost. You can also add $5 to any week to sponsor more. More on paying it forward.

Is there a contract?

No. It's week to week. The only thing we ask up front is an optional $25 refundable deposit to hold a founding spot in the pilot.
