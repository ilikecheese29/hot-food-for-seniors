Our story

It started with my grandma's dinners

从奶奶的晚餐说起。

My grandma doesn't have a smartphone, so she can't order food herself. Her personal support workers cook most of her meals, and a few nights a week my dad orders her dinner from a restaurant she likes.

It works, mostly. But I watched what it cost him. About $30 a meal on a delivery app. Time spent every week hunting for something good at a fair price. And the part that never went away: not knowing whether the food arrived hot, whether she opened the door, and whether she ate it.

When I started asking around, I heard the same thing from other families. The food was only half of it. What they wanted was to know their parent was okay.

What we're building

Warm Bowl is simple on purpose. A few evenings a week, the same trusted driver brings your parent a hot dinner from a restaurant they already love, says hello, and lets you know how it went.

We're starting with Chinese food in one neighbourhood, Bayview Hill in Richmond Hill. My grandparents live there and we visit often, so it's a community I know, and Chinese food is what my grandma loves. Keeping all our families on one short route is what keeps the food hot and the price fair.

Being honest about where we are

I'm a student, and this is a new, small business. For now an adult in my family drives, and I handle the orders, the restaurants, the families and the handoff at the door. Our first families will help shape how it works. I'd rather tell you that up front than pretend to be something bigger.

What I can promise is that every family gets my attention, every driver is someone we've checked and trained, and if something isn't working, we'll fix it or tell you plainly that we can't.

What we believe

  • Dignity first. It's a restaurant dinner, not "care." Your parent gets to choose what they eat.
  • The driver is the product. Patience and warmth matter more than speed.
  • Meals on Wheels is a friend, not a rival. If it's the better fit for your family, we'll tell you.
  • Say what we can't do. A note from the driver isn't proof your parent ate, and we won't pretend it is.
  • Give back as we go. For every 20 meals sold, a senior in need gets a free one.

Gerald

Founder, Warm Bowl
