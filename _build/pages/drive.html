What a shift looks like

  1. 4:30 pm: pick up

    Collect the evening's meals from one plaza. Check they're hot and pack the insulated bags.

  2. 5:00 pm: deliver

    Drive one short route in one neighbourhood, about 10 to 20 stops.

  3. At each door

    Hand the meal over in person, say hello, and notice if anything seems wrong.

  4. About 6:30 pm: done

    Send a short note for each stop. A shift is about 2 to 3 hours.

What you get

  • About $20 an hour, paid by the hour, not per delivery
  • Mileage paid on top
  • Set evenings, Tuesday and Thursday to start, that fit around school, work or retirement
  • The same route each time, so you get to know your seniors
  • Training on our safety steps and on dementia awareness

What you need

  • 18 or older, with a full G licence and your own car
  • A clean driving record
  • Willing to get a police vulnerable sector check
  • Car insurance that covers delivery, or willing to add it. We'll explain how.
  • Patience and warmth. Speaking Cantonese or Mandarin is a big plus.

A note on insurance

Most personal car insurance doesn't cover delivery. Before your first shift you'll need a delivery endorsement on your policy. Your insurer can add it, and we'll tell you what to ask for.

大多数个人汽车保险不涵盖送餐。第一次出车前，需要在保单上加上送餐附加险，我们会告诉您怎么跟保险公司说。
