We're looking for kind, reliable people with a car to deliver dinner to the same seniors in Richmond Hill each week. If you're patient, like talking to older people, and want a few steady hours, we'd like to hear from you.

The job

What a shift looks like

4:30 pm

Pick up the evening's meals from one plaza. Check temperatures and pack the hot bags.

5:00 pm

Drive one short route in one neighbourhood, about 10 to 20 stops.

At each door

Hand over the meal in person, say hello, and note anything unusual.

6:30 pm

Send your short notes and you're done. About 2 to 3 hours.

What you get

Fair, steady, and meaningful

  • About $20 an hour, paid hourly, not per delivery
  • Mileage paid on top
  • Set evenings, Tuesday and Thursday to start, that fit around school, work or retirement
  • The same route each time, so you get to know your seniors
  • Training on our safety protocol and dementia awareness

What we need

  • 18 or older, with a valid G licence and your own car
  • A clean driving record
  • Willing to complete a police vulnerable sector check
  • Car insurance that covers delivery, or willing to add it (we'll explain)
  • Patience and warmth. Cantonese or Mandarin is a big plus.

A note on insurance

Most personal car insurance doesn't cover delivery. Before your first shift you'll need a delivery endorsement on your policy. It's usually a phone call to your insurer, and we'll help you with what to ask.
