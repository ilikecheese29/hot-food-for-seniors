The food

Food they grew up with, cooked fresh nearby

家常味道，附近餐馆新鲜烹制。

Seniors who stop eating well often aren't short of food. They're short of food they actually want. So we start with the dishes your parent already loves, from restaurants close to home.

01 / Where it comes from

Two or three restaurants, one plaza

We partner with a few family restaurants in the same plaza in Bayview Hill, with one restaurant cooking each delivery night. Your parent gets variety, and the driver makes just one pickup, so every meal stays hot.

03 / Diet needs

Adjusted for older appetites

Tell us once, and every order is placed with it in mind. The restaurant confirms each order before it's cooked.

  • Low salt, low sugar or low fat
  • Soft texture for chewing or dental problems
  • Smaller portions, so less goes to waste
  • Allergies recorded and printed on every label
  • Spice level, and dishes they won't eat

A note on medical diets. We can't replace advice from your parent's doctor or dietitian. If they have a strict medical diet, talk to us first and we'll tell you honestly whether we can meet it.

05 / Not "takeout"

Home-style meals, not greasy takeout every night

We hear this worry a lot, and it's fair. That's why we start with a few treat dinners a week, not every meal, and why we pick dishes like congee, steamed fish and soups rather than the fried things that travel badly anyway.

Each partner restaurant will offer a lighter list: the same dishes with less salt, oil and sugar, softer textures and smaller portions. Where we can, a dietitian will look over that list and flag good choices for low-salt or diabetic seniors.

We'll always use real restaurants, never a central kitchen. Their cooking is what your parent misses, and it can't be copied.
