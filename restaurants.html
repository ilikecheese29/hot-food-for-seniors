Why partner

Different from a delivery app

Delivery apps take 15% to 30% of each order, and orders arrive at random. We work the other way around.

1

Orders the day before

Each partner gets its own fixed night every week. You get the full order list by 2 pm, often the night before, so you can prep in one batch.

2

A small, fixed menu

We agree five or six dishes with you, including lighter versions with less salt and softer textures. No surprises in the kitchen.

3

One pickup, paid on time

Our driver collects every order in one trip at 4:30 pm. Families pay us weekly in advance, so we pay you on time.

What we'd ask of you

  • A senior-size portion of a few dishes, at a set price per meal
  • Lighter versions where you can: less salt and oil, softer, smaller
  • Ingredients and allergens for each dish, for our labels
  • Meals hot and packed by a set time on your night
  • A heads-up if you'll be closed, so we can plan

Honest about volume

We start with a small test in spring 2027 and a summer pilot of 10 to 15 seniors, building toward 15 to 20 meals a night on one route. We'd rather be honest about our numbers than promise volume we can't deliver.

As the route fills, we'd like to agree a guaranteed nightly minimum with our partners. That steady volume is what makes a partner price work for both of us.

中文说明

餐厅合作

暖碗为列治文山 Bayview Hill 的长者送晚餐，餐点来自附近几家本地餐馆。我们正在寻找会做长者熟悉的家乡菜的合作餐厅。

外卖平台每单抽取15%至30%的佣金，订单也很随机。我们的做法不一样：

  • 提前下单：每家合作餐厅每周有固定的一晚。下午2点前（通常是前一晚）发给您完整订单，可以一次备齐。
  • 固定小菜单：与您商定五六道菜，包括少盐少油、较软的版本。
  • 一次取餐，按时付款：司机下午4:30一次取齐所有订单。家庭每周预付，所以我们能按时付款给您。

我们希望您可以

  • 提供几道长者份量的菜，每餐固定价格
  • 尽量提供少盐少油、较软、份量较小的版本
  • 告诉我们每道菜的成分和过敏原，用于餐盒标签
  • 在送餐当晚按时备好，保温包装
  • 如果休息，请提前通知我们

坦诚说明订单量

2027年春季小规模试运行，夏季试点约10至15位长者，目标是一条路线每晚15至20份。路线满了以后，我们希望和合作餐厅约定每晚的最低订单量。
