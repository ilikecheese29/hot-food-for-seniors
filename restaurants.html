For restaurants

Predictable orders, placed the day before, with no app commission

餐厅合作：提前一天下单，固定菜单，零平台佣金。

We bring dinner to seniors in Bayview Hill from a small group of local restaurants. We're looking for partners who cook the food seniors grew up with.

Why partner

Different from a delivery app

Delivery apps take 15% to 30% of each order, and orders arrive at random. We work the other way around.

1

Orders the day before

Each partner gets its own fixed night every week. You get the full order list by 2 pm, often the night before. Prep in one batch, not one ticket at a time.

2

A small, fixed menu

We agree five or six dishes with you, including softer and lower-salt versions. No surprises in the kitchen.

3

One pickup, paid on time

Our driver collects every order in one trip at 4:30 pm. Families pay us weekly in advance, so we pay you on time.

What we'd ask

A senior-friendly menu at a partner price

  • A senior-size portion of a few dishes, at a set price per meal
  • Lower-salt and softer versions where possible
  • Ingredients and allergens for each dish, for our labels
  • Meals ready, hot and packed by a set time on delivery days
  • A heads-up if you'll be closed, so we can plan

Honest about volume

Starting small, growing on one route

We start with a small test in spring 2027 and a summer pilot of 10 to 15 seniors, building toward 15 to 20 meals a night on one route. We'd rather be honest about our numbers at the start than promise volume we can't deliver.

As the route fills, we'd like to agree a guaranteed daily minimum with one main partner. That predictable volume is what makes a partner price work for both of us.
