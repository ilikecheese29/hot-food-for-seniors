Ask about it

Everyone on a route gets dinner on the same set nights. That's what keeps the route short, the food hot and the price at $20. Founding families keep the price they signed up at.

Every plan includes

What you're actually paying for

The meal is the easy part. What families tell us they want is to stop worrying. So these come with every plan, at no extra cost.

The same driver

A background-checked, familiar face at every visit.

A safety check

Our written no-answer protocol, followed every time.

A note to you

A short message after each delivery.

Clear labels

Name, dish, ingredients, allergens and diet flags.

Hot, or it's free

Any meal more than 30 minutes late, or cold, is refunded.

Your first week

Half price, with a full refund if it isn't right

Try it for a week at 50% off: $20 for 2 Treats, or $30 for 3 Treats, plus HST. If any meal is late or cold, you get that meal back in full.

Most families need a week to trust something new with a parent. That's what the trial is for.

Founding families

A spot in the pilot

The summer 2027 pilot has room for 10 to 15 seniors on one route, within about 3 km of our partner restaurants. Joining the waitlist is free, and there's nothing to pay until your first week.

Before the pilot starts, we'll message families on the waitlist to offer spots. Founding families keep the price they signed up at.

Pay it forward

One free meal for every 20 sold

For every 20 meals families buy, we give a free hot dinner to a senior in need, found through a local community agency or Meals on Wheels referrals. Same driver, same check-in, no charge to them.

Payment

How billing works

When you're charged

Weekly, in advance, by card, every Sunday for the coming week. You never get chased for payments.

Who pays

Whoever sets it up, usually a son or daughter. Your parent never handles money with the driver.

Skipped meals

Cancel before 2 pm on the day and it's credited to next week. Hospital stays are paused at no charge.

Tax

Prices shown are before HST. A $20 meal comes to $22.60 with tax.

Later on

Everyday plans

As routes fill, we'll add more partner restaurants and more nights, up to dinner every night, and then weekday lunches. Every meal is still real restaurant food, at the same $20. If restaurants give us better prices as we grow, we'll pass the savings on as a small discount on these plans, or as more free meals. These are planned, not yet available.
